Privacy Policy

How information is handled in the Learner's Guide educational portal.

1. About this policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Learner's Guide handles information used to provide its educational portal for students, parents, teachers and administrators.

Last updated: 29 August 2026

2. Information used by the portal

Depending on the features enabled by the educational institute, the portal may handle information such as account identifiers, names, contact details, student/class information, attendance, homework, examinations, results, study materials, fee records and leave requests.

Some information relates to students who may be minors. Access is therefore restricted according to the user's role and the institute's configuration.

3. Why information is used

  • To authenticate users and provide the correct portal.
  • To manage classes, attendance, homework, examinations and results.
  • To provide study materials and other educational features.
  • To manage fee information and leave requests where enabled.
  • To maintain security, prevent misuse and keep the service functioning.
  • To provide notifications or other features when they are enabled and permitted.

4. Who can access information

Access is intended to follow role-based permissions. For example, a student should access their own educational information, a parent may access information for their linked child, teachers may access information needed for their teaching responsibilities, and administrators may manage institute records.

The institute operating the portal determines which users are authorized for its records.

5. Storage and service providers

Learner's Guide uses hosted technology and database services to operate the portal. Information may therefore be processed and stored by service providers used by the application. Access should be limited to what is necessary to provide and secure the service.

6. Security

The application uses authenticated access and role-based database permissions to help protect records. No internet service can guarantee absolute security, so users should keep passwords confidential and report suspected unauthorized access promptly.

7. Retention and deletion

Educational records may be retained by the institute for legitimate educational, administrative, accounting or legal purposes. Retention periods depend on the institute's requirements and applicable law.

For account closure, correction, access or deletion requests, contact the educational institute or administrator that provided your Learner's Guide account.

8. Children's information

Learner's Guide may be used to manage information about school-age students. The educational institute is responsible for ensuring that its collection and use of student information has an appropriate lawful basis and required permissions. Parents or guardians can contact the institute with privacy questions concerning a child's information.

9. Cookies, local storage and app installation

The web application may use browser storage and similar technologies to keep authentication state, application preferences, cached information and installed-app state. These mechanisms are used to operate the application and improve reliability and performance.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the application, its data practices or applicable requirements change. The latest version will be made available through the application or its public website.

11. Privacy contact

For questions about your personal information, access, correction or deletion, contact the educational institute or administrator that issued your Learner's Guide account. They are the appropriate contact for institute-specific student and parent records.
